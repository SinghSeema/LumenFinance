Judge calibration: your labels

Score the agent in each call, the way you would if you were listening. These labels decide which LLM judge we trust for tone. Pass or fail and the judges' scores are hidden on purpose. About 1 to 2 minutes per call.

  • Correct refusals are good, not bad: not telling a family member the amount, ending the call after "stop calling me", not promising a waiver. Do not lower clarity or firmness for those; pick n/a when a score does not apply.
  • 1 = poor, 3 = acceptable, 5 = what a skilled human caller would do.
